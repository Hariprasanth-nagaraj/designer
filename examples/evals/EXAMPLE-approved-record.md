## Design hypothesis

A SHIPPED EXAMPLE. Professional operators scanning many live items need peripheral
change detection, so the direction prioritises a dense operations table with a
scarce accent reserved for selection and the single committed action.

## References used — and why each was relevant

EXAMPLE: extracted relationship-level guidance (density, type hierarchy, surface
strategy) only. No palette, wordmark, voice or layout was copied.

## What worked — keep this

EXAMPLE: tabular numerals, a signed-P&L pairing of glyph plus colour, and a hairline
surface ladder that made the data table the loudest thing on the screen.

## System decisions that earned their keep

EXAMPLE: one radius family; borders instead of shadows for grouping; a per-instrument
decimal precision rule so a real tick is never rounded away.

## Why this is worth reusing

EXAMPLE: the user recognised the domain instantly and raised no density comments.
Reuse the reasoning pattern, not the palette.
