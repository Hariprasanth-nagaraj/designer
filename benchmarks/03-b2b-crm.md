# Benchmark 3 — B2B CRM

**Fixed brief.**

Design the pipeline view for a B2B sales team (12 reps, 3 segments) using a CRM they
did not choose. Reps live here; managers use it in a Monday review; leadership looks
at it monthly.

Must support: scan the pipeline; open and edit a deal; see activity history; log an
activity; move a deal stage; and forecast.

## Why this brief
The most likely candidate for generic-AI-SaaS output (a CRM is the canonical "make me
a table with some cards" request). Tests whether the system resists kanban-ification
and card grids, and whether it models the actual job (stages, ownership, forecast
uncertainty).

## Rubric
| Dimension | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Product reasoning | Stage semantics, ownership, forecast — or just a status column? |
| IA | Is the pipeline a table, a board, or both? Is the choice justified? |
| Colour | Is the accent scarce, or is every stage a colour? |
| Typography | Dense numeric hierarchy for deal values and dates? |
| Density | Compact rows beside an airy detail panel? |
| Components | One card philosophy, applied? |
| Craft | No kanban-by-default, no coloured pills per stage without meaning? |
| Flow | Scan → open deal → log activity → advance stage → forecast updates? |
