# Benchmark 1 — High-density trading console

**Fixed brief (do not vary between system versions).**

Design the primary screen of a bullion and metals trading terminal for professional
traders who monitor 20+ live markets during a 10-hour session, act on price moves
within seconds, and cannot afford a misleading number.

Must support: live multi-instrument watchlist; a price/market view; an order ticket
with maker-checker approval; position and P&L; and a way to reach settlement details
without losing the current market context.

The screen will be used on a 1440px trading floor monitor and glanced at, not studied.

## Why this brief
Highest density + highest trust + highest time-pressure. It is the sharpest test of
whether the system can produce an *operational* interface rather than a card grid.

## Rubric
| Dimension | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Product reasoning | Does it know a trader scans, not reads? Is the ticker/price the hero? |
| IA | Is order entry reachable in one action? Is approval modelled, not implied? |
| Colour | Is one accent scarce and meaningful? Are positive/negative states non-colour-cued? |
| Typography | Tabular numerals? Dense body type that stays legible at 13px? |
| Density | One consistent density across nav, rows, controls, containers? |
| Geometry | Restrained radii, no pill filters, no oversized cards? |
| Surfaces | One hierarchy mechanism, applied consistently? |
| Components | Tables for repetitive data. Cards only where containment matters? |
| Craft | Alignment, rhythm, no generic-AI tells? |
| Flow | Can a trader go watchlist → ticket → approve → verify P&L without losing context? |
