# Benchmark 5 — Knowledge and research product

**Fixed brief.**

Design the reading and synthesis view for a research tool used by graduate students
and analysts: collect sources, read them, take notes, and produce a written synthesis
with citations.

Must support: read a document; highlight and annotate; see notes alongside the source;
search across a library; and export a cited outline.

## Why this brief
Tests editorial typography, long-form line length and rhythm, restraint, and the
ability to design a two-pane workspace without it becoming a card grid.

## Rubric
| Dimension | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Product reasoning | Does it know the reading is the product? |
| IA | Source ↔ note relationship modelled? |
| Colour | Warm editorial or cool tool — is the choice argued? |
| Typography | Serif for reading, sans for chrome — clearly distinct, or a mess? |
| Density | Reading measure vs workspace chrome — is the difference intentional? |
| Surfaces | Two panes separated by tone/border, not by shadow soup? |
| Flow | Open source → annotate → note appears beside it → search → export with citations? |
